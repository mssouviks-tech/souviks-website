# NCH convergence registration readiness — Souviks

**Status:** Prepared for owner completion; not submitted  
**Assessment date:** 3 November 2026  
**Target:** Complete registration and retain NCH acceptance before 1 January 2027.

The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026 require every e-commerce entity to become a partner in the National Consumer Helpline (NCH) convergence process from 1 January 2027. This applies to Souviks' direct online inventory-selling model; the requirement is not limited to marketplaces.

## Official NCH process

- Registration landing page: https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/public/convergenc-step-one
- Registration form: https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/public/convergencepartnersreq
- The NCH page currently lists two uploads: **Authorization Letter** and **Company Undertaking Letter**. Download the current versions from the official page and use those templates rather than a locally recreated form.
- The registration form asks for the registered company name, ROC/CIN status where applicable, company profile, industry, CEO/MD contact, head-office address, head of customer service, a specific point of contact (SPOC), customer-care details, the two letters and the authorised signatory.
