# Part Identification

Use the Souviks catalogue to locate products from identifiers or descriptive text.

## Search order

1. Exact part number or MPN.
2. Exact SKU.
3. Product name.
4. Brand and category.
5. Broader catalogue search terms.

Use https://www.souviks.co.in/assets/data/products.json for structured product records and https://www.souviks.co.in/assets/data/search.json for search-oriented discovery.

Do not infer an exact part match from a visually similar or partially matching identifier. Report ambiguity when multiple records match.
