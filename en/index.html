About Souviks
Catalogue overview

Genuine OEM & Premium Aftermarket Commercial Vehicle Spare Parts

Souviks supplies commercial vehicle spare parts for trucks and buses across India, with a catalogue covering clutch parts, bearings, filters, brake components, electrical parts, engine parts, lubricants, suspension and steering components. Customers can browse products by brand, category, vehicle or part number and request availability, pricing and compatibility assistance. Our catalogue includes genuine OEM and premium aftermarket parts from established brands, supported by Souviks' experience serving workshops, retailers, fleet operators and distributors since 1986.

Souviks is your trusted destination for genuine OEM and premium aftermarket commercial vehicle spare parts. Serving retailers, workshops, fleet operators and distributors across India.
