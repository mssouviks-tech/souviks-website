Description

Description

The RKI K14EX Cross Holder L/L 2214 locates and supports the cross-joint assembly within the connected driveline yoke. The specified configuration includes 8 holes of 10.2 mm, a 17-bolt pattern, a 130 mm plate, a height of 113 mm and cross dimensions of 41.27 × 126. Vehicle applications include Tata LPT and Ashok Leyland 2516.
