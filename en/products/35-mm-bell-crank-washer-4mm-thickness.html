Description

The Harish 35 mm Bell Crank Washer 4mm Thickness sits between a fastener and the mating surface to distribute clamping load and protect the joint from local surface damage. Vehicle applications include Tata 407, Tata 709 and Tata 1109. Compatible vehicle references: Tata 407, Tata 709, Tata 1109, Tata LPT, Ashok Leyland 2516.
