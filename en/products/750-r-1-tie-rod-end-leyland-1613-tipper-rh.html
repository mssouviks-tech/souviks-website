Description

Description

The Alok 750-R-1 right-hand tie rod end is designed for Leyland 1613 Tipper steering linkage applications. It features an M30 × 1.5 right-hand threaded connection, an M20 × 1.5 ball-pin thread and a 30 mm spanner size. The component transfers steering movement through the linkage in heavy commercial vehicles. Compatible vehicle references: ashok-leyland-2516.

Applications

  • Leyland 1613 Tipper steering linkage
  • M30 × 1.5 RH thread; M20 × 1.5 ball-pin thread
