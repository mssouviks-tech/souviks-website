Description

Description

The RKI K478 Cross Holder Flange Yoke – SPR 2040 locates and supports the cross-joint assembly within the connected driveline yoke. Its specified configuration includes a 47.68 × 135 cross, a 150 mm plate, 4 holes of 13.0 mm and a 19-bolt pattern. Vehicle applications include Ashok Leyland U Truck and Tata LPT. Compatible vehicle references: Ashok Leyland U-Truck, Tata LPT.
