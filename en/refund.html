Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains when a refund may be available, how refund requests are reviewed, and how approved refunds are processed by Souviks.

Effective date: 1 September 2026

Refund Policy Overview

Souviks processes refunds only in accordance with the applicable order terms, Refund Policy, product-specific conditions and applicable law.

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be processed only after the relevant product has been received and inspected, unless the circumstances or applicable law require otherwise.

When a Refund May Be Available

A refund may be available where an eligible product is received damaged, is confirmed to have a manufacturing defect, or where Souviks is otherwise required to provide a refund under the applicable order terms or law.

  • Visible damage at delivery must be reported within 1 hour of delivery.
  • Eligible return or exchange requests generally must be raised within 7 days of delivery.
  • The product must satisfy the applicable return and inspection conditions.
  • The refund request must be approved by Souviks after verification, except where applicable law provides otherwise.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact Souviks using the customer-support details provided on this page and provide the order or invoice number, customer details, product information and reason for the request.

Souviks may request photographs, videos, packaging details, product markings or other information reasonably necessary to assess the claim.

Inspection and Verification

Refund claims may require the affected product to be returned to Souviks for inspection and verification.

The inspection may consider the product condition, packaging, seals, identifying markings, evidence of installation or use, and the nature and cause of the reported damage or defect.

Refund Approval

A refund is not automatically approved merely because a request has been submitted. Souviks will assess the claim against the applicable policy and circumstances.

  • Approved for refund.
  • Approved for replacement or exchange instead of refund, where appropriate and legally permitted.
  • Rejected where the claim does not meet the applicable policy or where the product has been damaged, altered or used in a manner that excludes the claim, subject to applicable law.

Refund Processing

For an approved return-based refund, Souviks will process the refund after the returned product has been received and inspected, unless a different process is required by applicable law or agreed for the particular transaction.

After Souviks initiates the refund, the time required for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment method, bank, card issuer or payment-service provider.

  • Refunds for prepaid orders will generally be made through the original payment channel where technically and operationally possible.
  • Where the original payment channel cannot be used, Souviks may request verified bank-account or other payment details necessary to complete the refund securely.
  • Souviks will not request unnecessary payment credentials, passwords, PINs or one-time authentication codes for processing a refund.

Refund Method

Souviks may accept UPI, bank transfer, net banking, credit or debit cards, cash and cash on delivery, subject to the payment options available for the order.

For cash-on-delivery or other transactions where there is no original electronic payment channel available, Souviks may process an approved refund through a verified bank account or another appropriate method.

Order Cancellation and Refunds

Where cancellation is available before dispatch, an eligible cancellation may result in a refund after verification and subject to the applicable payment and order conditions.

Cancellation is not permitted after dispatch. After dispatch, any eligible claim must be handled under the applicable return, exchange, refund or warranty process.

Non-Refundable Products and Situations

Certain products and situations are not eligible for refunds under Souviks' standard policy, subject always to any mandatory rights provided by applicable law.

  • Lubricants are non-returnable and non-refundable.
  • Electrical and electronic products are subject to their specific return conditions and are eligible for refund only where the applicable conditions are satisfied.
  • Products that have been installed, used, modified, dismantled, tampered with or returned with a broken applicable seal may be ineligible for a refund.

Statutory and Other Exceptions

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive any consumer right, statutory remedy or other protection that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Indian law. Where applicable law requires a refund, replacement or other remedy, Souviks will comply with that requirement.

Refund Support

For refund requests, refund status, or questions regarding an approved or pending refund, please contact Souviks.
