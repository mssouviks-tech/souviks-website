OUR STORY

A Legacy Built On Trust Since 1986

For nearly four decades, Souviks has supplied genuine and aftermarket commercial vehicle spare parts to workshops, retailers and fleet operators across India.

What began as a local spare parts business has grown into a trusted name known for reliability, product knowledge and consistent customer support.

Today we stock filters, bearings, clutch parts, brake components, lubricants, electricals, engine parts and thousands of related products for trucks and buses.

Genuine OEM and aftermarket — clearly distinguished

Souviks lists genuine/OE-specified products where the catalogue identifies them as genuine, and separately lists branded aftermarket products. An aftermarket product is not presented as an OEM part merely because it fits the same application.

Buying and distribution support

We help customers identify parts using part numbers, vehicle models, applications and product photographs. Orders can be supplied across India; availability, pricing and compatibility should be confirmed for the specific requirement before dispatch.

Frequently asked questions

Can Souviks help identify a part?

Yes. Send the part number, vehicle details or a clear photograph and the team can help match the requirement against the catalogue.

Do you supply genuine and aftermarket parts?

Yes. The catalogue contains both genuine/OE-specified and branded aftermarket products. They are not represented as the same thing.

Where do you ship?

Souviks serves customers across India. Delivery timing and freight treatment depend on the order and destination.
