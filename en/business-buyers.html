Frequently asked questions

Does Souviks accept wholesale enquiries?

Yes. Retailers, regional dealers, workshops, fleet maintenance teams and parts wholesalers can submit requirements. Include your business name, parts list and delivery destination.

Do you offer wholesale or volume pricing?

Each quotation is prepared against the requested parts and quantities. Any volume pricing, minimum quantity, payment terms, freight and dispatch estimate are confirmed for that quotation; do not assume a standard discount or minimum order.

Will every product shown online be in stock?

Not necessarily. The website is a catalogue, not a live inventory guarantee. Availability is checked for the specific enquiry.

Can you help without a part number?

Send vehicle make/model, engine or fuel variant if known, and a clear photo of the part or label. Exact application should be confirmed before ordering.

How should I send a longer parts list?

Use the RFQ basket for listed products. For a longer list, email info@souviks.co.in or send it through WhatsApp; include quantities and delivery PIN code.
